Direct, sealed and yours.

Straight from you to them

Every Mininotes is a node on Maxima, the peer-to-peer network of Minima. Notes travel device to device, sealed end to end, held only by the devices you share them with.

You hold the keys

Your address is a key made on your device and kept there. Open the app and start writing in seconds.

Proof on Minima

Stamp a note or a file on the Minima blockchain: proof of what it said and when, that anyone can check, for good.

Notes travel through public relays when your devices cannot reach each other. Run your own server and they go through a machine you control, which can keep your sealed backups too.

Roadmap

Where Mininotes comes from, where it is today, and where it is going.

  1. September 2026

    The first note crosses

    Work starts. A note written on one phone appears on another, sealed, over Maxima. A Windows app follows, then the lock with its 12 recovery words, and the code is published as free software.

  2. October 2026

    A place for everything

    Folders inside folders, pictures and files, a Home screen like a phone's. Sharing with roles and with groups of people, and a Temp folder that sends things between your own devices.

  3. Now

    Testing with the Minima community

    People in the Minima community use Mininotes every day and tell us what breaks and what is missing. Every report shapes the next build. Join in.

  4. Next

    On F-Droid

    Mininotes listed on F-Droid, built from its public source, so anybody can install and update it without us in between.

  5. After that

    An app for everybody

    Out of the community and into everyday use: a pad anyone can pick up, for a family, a team or a group of friends, with nothing to know about what carries it.
